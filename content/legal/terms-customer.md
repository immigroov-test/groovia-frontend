Immigroov is committed to fostering a respectful, inclusive, and positive environment for all users. These guidelines outline the expectations for customer behavior while interacting with mentors and using the Immigroov platform.

1. Respectful Interaction

Treat mentors and platform support staff with courtesy, patience, and professionalism.
Avoid offensive, discriminatory, or aggressive language.
Respect cultural, religious, and personal differences.
2. Honest and Transparent Communication

Provide accurate information when creating your account and booking a session.
Share genuine concerns and expectations when prompted during the booking process.
Avoid misrepresenting your intentions or identity.
3. Punctuality and Participation

Join scheduled sessions on time.
Notify your mentor as early as possible if you are unable to attend.
Refrain from being idle, distracted, or disruptive during the session.
4. Prohibited Conduct

Customers may not:

Solicit mentors for off-platform services or communication
Harass, threaten, or intimidate mentors or other users
Record sessions without explicit permission from the mentor
Share any mentor’s personal information or materials outside the session
5. Feedback and Ratings

Provide honest, constructive feedback and ratings based on your experience.
Avoid leaving retaliatory or intentionally misleading reviews.
If you encounter issues, contact Immigroov support instead of using ratings as a complaint channel.
6. Data Privacy and Security

Do not ask mentors to disclose their private or financial information.
Keep your login credentials confidential and secure.
Use the platform only for its intended purpose and not for data harvesting or scraping.
7. Consequences of Violation

Immigroov reserves the right to:

Warn, suspend, or permanently ban users who violate these guidelines
Remove any content, reviews, or messages that breach the standards outlined herein
Report serious misconduct to relevant legal or regulatory authorities

If you experience inappropriate behavior or suspect a violation of these guidelines, please contact us immediately at support@immigroov.com.
