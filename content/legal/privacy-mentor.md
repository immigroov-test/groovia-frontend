## Data Controller

Immigroov acts as the Data Controller for all personal data collected from Mentors during registration, onboarding, and platform usage.

## Data We Collect

We collect and process the following data from Mentors:

- Full name
- Email address and phone number
- Country and location
- Government-issued identity verification documents
- Profile photo, bio, expertise category, and social media links (optional)
- Session calendar, availability, and booking preferences
- Ratings, reviews, and session performance data
- Banking details for payout processing (via secure channels)

## Purpose of Processing

The collected data is used for:

- Account creation and verification
- Mentor profile listing and public visibility on the platform
- Session scheduling and availability management
- Payout calculation, disbursement, and taxation compliance
- Monitoring performance and session quality
- Communication regarding platform updates, support, or disputes
- Legal compliance (e.g., recordkeeping, audits)

## Legal Basis

Processing is based on:

- Contractual necessity (for service delivery)
- Legitimate interests (platform operation, fraud prevention)
- Legal obligations (recordkeeping and compliance)
- Explicit consent (for marketing or testimonials)

## Cookies and Session Analytics

Immigroov uses cookies and session tracking tools to improve platform experience, security, and performance. This includes:

- Session identifiers
- Device/browser data
- Pages viewed and session duration
- Navigation behavior

Cookies are:

- Essential (for login, session booking)
- Analytics-based (for platform optimization)

You may disable cookies in your browser settings, but some features may not function properly.

## Data Retention

We retain your data:

- As long as your Mentor account remains active
- For up to 90 days post-termination for compliance and recordkeeping
- Ratings and reviews may remain visible post-termination unless deletion is requested

## Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal data. We may share data with:

- Payment processors and financial partners (for payout facilitation)
- Identity verification providers (if used)
- Legal or regulatory authorities upon request
- Technical vendors (for cloud hosting, analytics, or session scheduling tools)

All third-party providers are bound by appropriate data protection agreements.

## International Transfers

Your data may be stored or processed on servers outside your home country (e.g., EU, India). All transfers comply with GDPR Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your Rights

You may:

- Request access to your personal data
- Request correction or deletion of incorrect data
- Withdraw consent for optional processing
- Request data portability (where applicable)
- Object to certain types of processing (e.g., marketing)

To exercise these rights, email: support@immigroov.com. We respond within 30 days.

## Security

We use industry-standard encryption, role-based access controls, and secure infrastructure to safeguard your data.
