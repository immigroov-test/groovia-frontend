## What Data We Collect

We collect and process the following information when you use the Immigroov platform:

- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Country and time zone
- Booking preferences, session goals, and optional form responses
- Communication and support queries
- Feedback and session ratings
- Booking and payment confirmations (via third-party processors)

## How We Use Your Data

We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- Account creation and session bookings
- Sending session confirmations, reminders, and feedback links
- Facilitating communication with your selected Mentor
- Providing customer support
- Generating anonymized analytics for platform improvement
- Legal and tax compliance

## Legal Basis for Processing

Data is processed under the following lawful bases:

- Performance of contract (e.g., booking and delivering a session)
- Legitimate interests (platform operations and fraud prevention)
- Consent (for marketing or optional surveys)
- Legal obligations (e.g., financial records)

## Use of Cookies and Session Analytics

Immigroov uses essential and performance-enhancing cookies to:

- Maintain user sessions and security
- Optimize website navigation and page performance
- Track session duration and page views for analytics

Types of cookies used:

- Essential cookies: Required for login, booking, and platform functionality
- Analytics cookies: Help track how users navigate the platform for UX improvements

You may disable non-essential cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may impair platform functionality.

## Data Retention

We retain your data:

- For as long as your account remains active

- Anonymized usage and rating data may be retained for analytical purposes

## Data Sharing

Immigroov does not sell your personal data. We may share your data with:

- Mentors you book sessions with (limited to name, time zone, and session responses)
- Payment processors for session fee transactions (no full payment details are stored by Immigroov)

- Regulatory bodies when required by law

All third-party vendors are GDPR-compliant and under binding agreements.
