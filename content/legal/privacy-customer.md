We process your personal data for the following purposes:

- Account creation and session bookings

- Facilitating communication with your selected Mentor
- Providing customer support
- Generating anonymized analytics for platform improvement

Data is processed under the following lawful bases:

## AI-Assisted Features and Automated Processing

Immigroov is an AI-assisted platform. Groovia, our AI assistant, answers questions and can generate a career pathway report. You are told this before you use it, and AI-generated answers carry an on-screen notice that they are informational and are not legal or immigration advice.

**Uploading a resume is optional and always asked for separately.** We ask for your explicit agreement at the moment you upload, using a tick box that is never pre-ticked, and we record that agreement together with its date and the version of the wording you agreed to. Agreeing to cookies is not agreeing to this, and we do not treat it as such. If you do not agree, the resume is not uploaded and is not processed.

When you do agree:

- Your resume file is read and converted to text. We do not keep the original file.
- The extracted text is processed by a third-party AI model provider acting as our processor, and is used to generate your career pathway report and to suggest relevant Mentors.
- The text and your conversation with Groovia are retained as part of your chat history so that your report and follow-up questions remain available to you.
- Your resume is not shared with Mentors. Mentors receive only the details listed under Data Sharing.

Groovia produces information and suggestions for you to act on. It does not make automated decisions that produce legal effects concerning you, and no visa, immigration or eligibility decision is made by our platform.

You may withdraw your agreement at any time by contacting us at the address below, and you may ask us to delete your resume data under Your Rights.

All third-party vendors are GDPR-compliant and under binding agreements.
